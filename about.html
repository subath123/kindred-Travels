Our Sri Lankan Heritage

Kindred Travels was founded in Colombo by a small collective of conservationists, historical researchers, and high-end hospitality veterans. Our goal was simple: to move away from rigid, cookie-cutter tourism packages and return to the golden age of bespoke travel discovery.

We believe that to truly feel the heartbeat of Sri Lanka, you must step off the crowded highways. Whether it is tracking a leopard footprint alongside a veteran park ranger in Yala, enjoying private cooking lessons with spice curators in Kandy, or waking up to the mist of Ella rolling past a luxury mountain lodge, we craft every day-by-day path from scratch.

Our Mission

To design bespoke, luxury travel experiences that respect local ecosystems, celebrate heritage, and directly fund regional community conservation projects.

Our Vision

To be the premier conscious travel agency in South Asia, setting the industry benchmark for client care, personalized logistics, and sustainable footprint.

Our Founding Pillars

1. Absolute Customization

No two paths are the same. We take time to understand your tastes, hobbies, and style to construct a tailored daily route just for you.

2. Ecological Conservation

We work exclusively with green hotels, ban plastics in our tour vehicles, and donate a portion of all bookings to Yala ranger units.

3. Curatorial Integrity

Our guides are licensed, local, and experienced historians or naturalists, providing insightful, respectful cultural translation.
