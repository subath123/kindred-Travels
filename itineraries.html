Classic Ceylon Highlights

A perfect introductory loop taking you through the cultural core, scenic hill country, and historic colonial coasts.

7 Days / 6 Nights
First-timers
Day 1

Arrival & Galle Face Sunset Walk

Land at Colombo Airport. Transfer to your boutique city hotel and walk along Galle Face Green at sunset.

Day 2

Dambulla Caves to Sigiriya

Drive to the Cultural Triangle. Visit the massive golden cave temples of Dambulla, then settle into your jungle chalet.

Day 3

Conquering Sigiriya (Lion Rock)

Scale the sheer 1,200 steps of Sigiriya Fortress at sunrise. Transfer to Kandy, the majestic royal hill capital.

Day 4

Tooth Relic Temple & Tea Fields Train

Visit Kandy's Tooth Temple. Settle into the observation car of the scenic train winding up to the tea estate valleys of Ella.

Day 5

Colonial Walk of Galle Fort

Descend the mountains to the coastal ramparts of Galle Fort. Wander its Dutch architecture, lighthouse, and art studios.

Day 6

Mirissa Beach & Whale Watching

Unwind on Mirissa's crescent beach. Catch a boat to view giant blue whales or snorkel along the coastal turtle reefs.

Day 7

Souvenirs & Departure

Drive back to Colombo for final souvenir shopping before transferring to the airport for your flight home.

Highlands & Wildlife Quest

Deepen your connection with Sri Lanka’s raw wilderness, tracking leopards in Yala, spotting elephants, and trekking cloud forests.

10 Days / 9 Nights
Nature Lovers
Day 1

Negombo Coastal Settle-in

Arrive and transfer to a relaxing beachfront resort in Negombo. Recover from jet lag by the pool.

Day 2

Minneriya wild Elephant Gathering

Drive to Minneriya for a 4x4 open-top safari. Witness hundreds of wild elephants drinking at the lake edge.

Day 3

Sigiriya Fortress Climb

Conquer Sigiriya rock fortress. Later, enjoy a traditional catamaran ride and clay-pot lunch in Hiriwadunna.

Day 4

Kandyan Dance & Tooth Relic Temple

Explore Kandy's royal palace grounds, and attend an evening Kandyan percussion and fire dance showcase.

Day 5

Trekking Horton Plains & Nuwara Eliya

Rise early to hike the misty Horton Plains to World's End cliff. Warm up with tea in Nuwara Eliya.

Day 6

Scenic Train to Ella Gap

Board the scenic blue train to Ella. Climb Little Adam's Peak for sunset views over the valleys.

Day 7

Leopard Tracking in Yala National Park

Descend the hills to Yala. Take an afternoon safari to track leopards, elephants, and crocodiles.

Day 8

Elephant Transit Home & Galle

Visit Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home to watch feeding time. Proceed to Galle Fort to explore ramparts.

Day 9

Whale Safari & Turtle Snorkeling

Embark on a whale expedition in Mirissa. Snorkel with giant sea turtles in Weligama's shallow bay.

Day 10

Galle to Colombo Airport

Transfer to the airport via the southern expressway, reflecting on ten days of wild adventure.

Grand Ceylon Discovery

The ultimate journey. Spans the ancient cultural ruins, the misty mountain hiking peaks, eastern coast beaches, safaris, and wellness spas.

14 Days / 13 Nights
Complete Tour
Day 1

Arrival in Colombo

Settle into your hotel. Join your private guide for a welcome dinner.

Day 2

Ancient Ruins of Anuradhapura

Explore the ruins and giant brick stupas of the island's first ancient kingdom.

Day 3

Sigiriya Fortress Climb

Conquer Lion Rock Fortress, then learn local cooking techniques at a farm.

Day 4

Polonnaruwa Archaeological Cycle

Rent a bicycle to explore the stone shrines, ruins, and reservoirs of Polonnaruwa.

Day 5

Trincomalee & Nilaveli Beach

Travel to the east coast and relax on the white sands of Nilaveli.

Day 6

Snorkeling Pigeon Island

Snorkel with green sea turtles and blacktip reef sharks in the marine reserve.

Day 7

Dambulla Caves to Kandy

Visit Dambulla cave temples. Settle in Kandy and attend a sacred Tooth temple ceremony.

Day 8

Peradeniya Royal Botanical Gardens

Stroll the lush botanic garden pathways famous for orchid houses and massive palms.

Day 9

Scenic Mountain Train Journey

Board the scenic train ride winding through highlands, tunnels, and pine forests.

Day 10

Trekking Ella Peak & Nine Arches

Hike Ella Peak for panoramic views. Visit the Nine Arch Bridge to watch the train cross.

Day 11

Udawalawe Elephant Sanctuary

Safari across Udawalawe reserve to spot massive herds of wild elephants.

Day 12

Galle Dutch Fort

Drive to Galle Fort. Walk colonial streets, boutique shops, and ramparts.

Day 13

Ayurvedic Wellness Spa

Enjoy an authentic oil massage, visit a sea turtle hatchery, and relax on Bentota beach.

Day 14

Colombo Tour & Departure

Sightsee Colombo's colonial buildings before transferring to the airport.

Tropical Beach Escape

A quick, sun-drenched getaway focusing on the colonial charm of Galle Fort and the gold beaches of the Southern Coast.

5 Days / 4 Nights
Relaxation
Day 1

Arrival & Galle Transfer

Land at BIA Colombo. Enjoy a direct expressway transfer to your historic boutique hotel inside Galle Fort.

Day 2

Galle Fort History & Weligama Surf

Explore the ramparts and Dutch church with a local guide. In the afternoon, try surfing on Weligama beach.

Day 3

Mirissa Whale Watching

Take an early morning cruise to spot Blue Whales. Spend the afternoon relaxing at Coconut Tree Hill.

Day 4

Madu River Safari & Bentota Spa

Enjoy a mangrove boat safari on Madu River, visit a cinnamon island, and end with an Ayurvedic foot massage.

Day 5

Colombo & Departure

Transfer back to Colombo for tea shopping and proceed to the airport for departure.
