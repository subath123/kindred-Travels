Into The Ceylon Wilds

Expert-led private game drives in Sri Lanka's most biodiverse wildlife sanctuaries.

Yala National Park Safari

Sri Lanka's flagship reserve. A stunning dry-zone scrub forest directly bordering the Indian Ocean, famous for holding one of the highest densities of leopards in the world, alongside massive tuskers and bears.

Key Wildlife:Leopards, Sloth Bears, Elephants, Peacocks
Best Months:February to June (Dry Season)
Terrain:Semi-arid scrub forest, coastal dunes
Inquire Safari Book / Plan

Udawalawe Elephant Sanctuary

A wide-open savannah surrounding a massive reservoir, reminiscent of East African plains. Guaranteed sightings of wild elephant herds feeding, bathing, and traversing the open wetlands.

Key Wildlife:Elephants, Water Buffaloes, Crocodiles, Eagles
Best Months:Year-Round (Best July to October)
Terrain:Savannah grasslands, river wetlands
Inquire Safari Book / Plan

Wilpattu Natural Forest

Sri Lanka's largest and oldest national park. Famed for its unique "Willus" (natural rainwater-filled lakes) that act as watering holes for wildlife inside a thick, majestic evergreen forest.

Key Wildlife:Leopards, Sloth Bears, Sambar Deer, Barking Deer
Best Months:February to October
Terrain:Thick dry-zone forest, natural sand rim lakes
Inquire Safari Book / Plan

Bundala Bird Sanctuary

A globally recognized Ramsar wetland. An absolute paradise for bird watchers, hosting thousands of colorful migratory birds, including massive flocks of Greater Flamingoes.

Key Wildlife:Greater Flamingoes, Pelicans, Painted Storks, Crocs
Best Months:September to March (Migratory peaks)
Terrain:Coastal lagoons, salt pans, scrub lands
Inquire Safari Book / Plan

Safari Game Timings

  • Morning Safari: 6:00 AM – 10:00 AM. (Best for active predators, cooler temperatures)
  • Evening Safari: 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM. (Best for elephants gathering at watering lakes)
  • Full-Day Expedition: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM. (Includes premium picnic lunch inside park resting zones)

4x4 Off-Road Vehicles

  • Customized raised open-sided Toyota Land Cruisers or Hiluxes.
  • Fitted with safety roll cages and canvas sun protection.
  • Limited to a maximum of 6 passengers per vehicle for window views.
  • Equipped with beanbags for camera lenses and reference books.

Critical Park Safety Regulations

  • Never disembark the vehicle: You must stay inside the safari jeep at all times, except in designated national park rest camp areas.
  • Keep absolute silence: Keep voice volume to a minimum when tracking to avoid disturbing leopards, cubs, and nesting birds.
  • Zero feeding or littering: Feeding wild animals is illegal. All snack packets and plastics must be kept inside the jeep trash bin.
  • No camera flashes: Switch off flash on cameras and smartphones, as it can startle elephants and provoke defensive behaviors.
