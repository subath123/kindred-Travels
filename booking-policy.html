Booking & Cancellation Policy

Deposit schedules, modification guidelines, and refund tiers for customized itineraries.

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

1. Securing Bookings & Deposit Schedules

To confirm and lock in your bespoke Sri Lankan travel itinerary, hotel rooms, and private transportation options, Kindred Travels requires a deposit payment.

  • Standard Package Deposit: A 30% non-refundable deposit is required to formally secure your tour reservation. Upon receiving this payment, our local curators initiate final confirmations with lodging and transport partners.
  • Specialized Booking Elements: Specific high-demand elements—such as domestic air taxi charters, boutique luxury tented camps (e.g. Leopard Trails or Kulu Safaris), train tickets (e.g. Ella Scenic Observation Rail), and peak-season hotel allocations—must be paid 100% in full at the time of reservation. These items are strictly non-refundable.

2. Cancellation Terms & Refund Tiers

If you wish to cancel your tour, you must submit a written notification via email to hello@kindredtravels.lk. The date of cancellation is determined by the day our team receives and acknowledges your email.

The initial 30% booking deposit is non-refundable in all circumstances. For the remaining 70% balance of your tour fee, the following cancellation refund tiers apply:

  • 60+ Days Prior to Tour Start Date: 70% refund of the remaining balance (excluding the non-refundable deposit and non-refundable specialized items).
  • 30 to 59 Days Prior to Tour Start Date: 50% refund of the remaining balance.
  • 15 to 29 Days Prior to Tour Start Date: 25% refund of the remaining balance.
  • Less than 15 Days Prior to Tour Start Date: 0% refund (no refund of any payments made).

3. Itinerary Modification Rules

Kindred Travels recognizes that travel plans can change. We allow modifications to confirmed dates or routes up to thirty (30) days prior to your arrival date.

Modifications are subject to availability, local hotel booking policies, and transport capacity. Kindred Travels charges a flat administrative fee of $50 USD per major itinerary change, plus any cost differences charged by local sub-contractors (e.g. seasonal room upgrades, transport adjustments, or flight changes).

Modifications requested less than thirty (30) days prior to arrival are treated as cancellations of the original booking and are subject to the standard cancellation tiers outlined above.

4. Force Majeure Exemptions

Force Majeure refers to unpredictable, unusual, and unavoidable events that are beyond the control of Kindred Travels. This includes monsoonal flash flooding, severe landslides, earthquakes, sudden airline route cancellations, border closures, pandemics, civil unrest, or official government travel warnings.

In the event that Force Majeure makes it physically impossible or legally unsafe to execute your scheduled tour, Kindred Travels is not liable to pay cash refunds. Instead, we will issue a travel credit voucher valid for 18 months from the cancellation date. This credit can be applied in full towards booking any future tour with us, subject to seasonal pricing variations.

5. Mandatory Travel Insurance

Because of the customized nature of high-end travel and the physical environments of Sri Lanka, Kindred Travels strictly requires all clients to purchase comprehensive travel insurance.

Your insurance policy must cover trip cancellations, travel delays, lost or damaged baggage, and emergency medical treatment, including emergency medical evacuation. You may be requested to present proof of your insurance policy to your private driver-guide at the start of your tour.
