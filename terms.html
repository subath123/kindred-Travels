Terms & Conditions

Agreement details, local regulations, and booking terms for our curated Sri Lankan tours.

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

1. Contractual Binding Agreement

By submitting an official booking request, approving a custom travel itinerary, or submitting payment details to Kindred Travels, you enter into a contractually binding legal agreement with us.

Your contract begins officially upon our generation of a booking confirmation invoice following receipt of your initial deposit. All agreements, tour arrangements, and legal disputes arising from this contract are strictly governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka.

2. Passport validity & Entry Visa

Travelers are solely responsible for ensuring they possess all necessary legal documentations required for entering Sri Lanka. Kindred Travels is not liable for entry denials, boarding issues, or travel disruptions due to documentation errors.

  • Passport Validity: Your passport must remain valid for a minimum duration of six (6) months starting from the date of your physical arrival in Sri Lanka.
  • Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA): All international travelers (excluding select passport holders with bilateral waivers) must secure an official Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) or tourist entry visa via the official Sri Lankan government portal prior to their flight departure.
  • Customs Frameworks: You must comply with local import-export customs regulations, specifically those concerning currency declarations and personal medication limits.

3. Local Regulations & Cultural Dress Guidelines

Sri Lanka is an island rich in cultural heritage and deep-rooted spiritual traditions. Respecting local laws and social customs is a critical requirement of joining our excursions.

  • Archaeological & Religious Sites: When visiting Buddhist temples, sacred ruins, or religious landmarks (e.g., Sigiriya Citadel sacred paths, Dambulla Cave Temple, or the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic in Kandy), conservative dress is mandatory. Shoulders and knees must remain fully covered. Footwear and headwear must be removed before entering temple compounds.
  • Buddhist Image Respect: It is illegal under Sri Lankan law to mistreat or show disrespect to images or statues of the Buddha. This includes posing for photographs with your back turned to a Buddha statue, attempting to climb or touch statues, or exhibiting visible tattoos depicting Buddhist imagery. Non-compliance can lead to arrest by local law enforcement.
  • Drone Photography: Drone use is heavily restricted. Operating a drone is strictly illegal unless you have secured prior written clearances from the Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka (CAASL) and the Ministry of Defense.
  • Safaris & Animal Welfare: During jeep safaris in Yala, Wilpattu, or Minneriya national parks, you must remain inside the designated vehicle cabin at all times. Feeding wildlife, littering, shouting, or teasing animals is strictly forbidden and results in immediate removal from the reserve.

4. Health Requirements & Physical Excursions

Our active adventure programs, which include hiking through Ella, climbing Adams Peak (Sri Pada), water rafting in Kitulgala, or scuba diving in Trincomalee, require standard levels of cardiorespiratory health and motor fitness.

You must declare any pre-existing medical conditions, cardiorespiratory limitations, severe allergies, or mobility issues to our team during the design phase of your custom itinerary. Kindred Travels is not liable for personal injuries, illnesses, or medical complications arising from hiking or high-altitude excursions if those conditions were not formally disclosed beforehand.

We require all clients to obtain comprehensive international medical travel insurance, which must specifically include emergency helicopter evacuation and high-altitude repatriation coverage.

5. Third-Party Service Providers & Liability Waivers

Kindred Travels designs itineraries by contracting and coordinating with independent third-party local entities. These entities include boutique hotels, private jeep safari operators, national rail lines, internal aviation charters, and regional catamaran operations.

Because we do not directly own, manage, or operate these hotels, transport fleets, or recreational facilities, Kindred Travels acts solely as a coordinating agency. We are not legally liable for any personal injury, property loss, delay, inconvenience, or damage caused by the omissions, defaults, bankruptcies, or negligent actions of these independent local sub-contractors.

6. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

This agreement is governed by the legal framework of Sri Lanka. Any formal claims, legal actions, or disputes arising from our services must be submitted in writing to our head office in Colombo within thirty (30) days of your tour's conclusion.

In the event of unresolved disputes, both parties agree to submit to the exclusive jurisdiction and arbitration of courts in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Arbitration proceedings will be conducted in English, with the costs shared equally between the parties.
