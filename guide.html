Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA)

All international visitors (except passport holders of Singapore, Maldives, and Seychelles) require an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) to enter Sri Lanka for tourism. You can apply easily online or obtain it upon arrival.

Step-by-Step Visa Guide

  1. Apply Online Before Flight: Visit the official government portal online to file your ETA application. Standard tourist ETAs grant 30 days of stay with double entry.
  2. Pay Processing Fees: Standard fees are $50 USD for most nationalities (South Asian SAARC countries pay $20 USD). Children under 12 are free.
  3. Receive ETA Email Approval: Processing takes 24 to 48 hours. Print out the approval confirmation letter sent to your email.
  4. Passport Validity Checklist: Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months from your date of arrival in Sri Lanka.
  5. Flight & Funds Proof: Have a printed copy of your return flight ticket out of Sri Lanka and sufficient funds for your stay.

Cultural Customs & Etiquette

Being mindful of local traditions goes a long way. Sri Lankans are warm, friendly, and hospitable people. Familiarize yourself with these core guidelines.

Do's

  • Greet people with "Ayubowan" (Sinhala) or "Vanakkam" (Tamil) with hands pressed together at chest level.
  • Dress respectfully when visiting temples: cover shoulders and knees, and remove shoes/hats before entering.
  • Use both hands (or only your right hand) when giving or receiving money, items, or food.
  • Tear roti or bread using only your right hand when eating traditional meals.

Don'ts

  • Do not take photographs standing with your back turned directly to a Buddha statue (highly offensive).
  • Do not touch or point with your feet, or touch anyone on the head (the head is considered sacred).
  • Do not take photos of military bases, checkpoints, or tea plantation workers without permission.
  • Do not sunbathe topless on beaches—modesty is highly valued off resort properties.
