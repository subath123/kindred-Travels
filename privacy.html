Privacy Policy

How we protect your Ceylon journey data, cookie guidelines, and local storage usage.

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

1. Introduction & Commitment

At Kindred Travels, registered under the commercial tourism framework of Sri Lanka, we are dedicated to safeguarding your personal privacy. We understand that organizing a premium, custom travel itinerary requires you to share sensitive personal and logistical details with us.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you browse our website, use our custom trip planner, or book travel packages across Sri Lanka. Our processes strictly comply with international data security standards, ensuring your tropical getaway remains secure from your very first click to your flight home.

2. Personal Data We Collect

To design, customize, and execute a bespoke Sri Lankan itinerary, we collect specific categories of personal data from our clients. This data collection takes place when you submit inquiries, complete our trip customizer wizard, or finalize bookings. The data collected includes:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full legal name, email address, home country, and telephone number. This allows us to coordinate with you and assign a dedicated local travel curator.
  • Logistical & Flight Records: Detailed flight arrival and departure times, flight numbers, and airline details. This is necessary to coordinate seamless airport transfers with your private chauffeur-guide.
  • Official Identification: Passport numbers, expiration dates, and full legal names as printed in your passport. We require these details to secure mandatory entrance clearances and permits for historical sites like Sigiriya Rock Fortress, or booking jeep permits for protected national parks like Yala, Wilpattu, and Udawalawe.
  • Specialized Preferences & Wellness Data: Dietary requirements, allergies, and physical fitness levels or medical restrictions. This information is passed on to our boutique lodging partners and specialized guides to prepare customized organic meals and structure trekking trails according to your capabilities.

3. Local Storage Usage

To provide a highly interactive and modern user experience, our website makes functional use of local storage (localStorage) on your web browser.

When you interact with our multi-step Trip Customizer Form Wizard, the temporary parameters you select—such as tentative travel dates, destination regions, selected interests, group sizes, and custom notes—are saved in your browser's local storage. This ensures that your customized draft itinerary is preserved if you accidentally close the tab, refresh the page, or lose your internet connection.

This data is stored entirely on your local device. Kindred Travels does not transmit or access this local storage data until you actively click the "Submit Inquiry" button to send your request to our curator team.

4. Cookie Guidelines & Analytics

Kindred Travels uses standard web cookies to monitor website performance, understand user interactions, and customize your browsing language. The cookies we utilize fall under the following categories:

  • Essential Functional Cookies: These cookies store session tokens and remember your language selector choice (e.g., keeping the page loaded in English, German, or French) as you navigate between subpages.
  • Analytical Cookies: We utilize services like Google Analytics to compile anonymous data regarding website traffic, identifying which Sri Lankan travel guides or custom routes receive the most interest. This helps us refine our content and user interface.
  • Marketing & Retargeting: We may occasionally use advertising pixels to show you seasonal Sri Lanka tour offers on external platforms. These cookies gather anonymous browser signatures only.

You have full control over cookie settings. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, disabling cookies may impact your ability to experience certain premium, personalized features of our site.

5. Information Security & Sharing Protocols

Your information is stored securely on protected databases using advanced Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption protocols.

To successfully execute your customized tour, Kindred Travels must share mandatory elements of your personal information with trusted local service partners:

  • Accommodation Providers: Surnames, nationalities, and room preferences are shared with booked hotels and boutique wellness resorts.
  • Transport Agencies & Chauffeurs: Flight numbers, names, and contact details are provided to your designated private driver to coordinate arrivals and pick-ups.
  • Wildlife & Heritage Authorities: Passport numbers are submitted to local agencies, such as the Department of Wildlife Conservation (DWC) or the Central Cultural Fund, to pre-purchase legal entry tickets for Sigiriya, Yala safaris, or Temple of the Tooth ceremonies.

We strictly enforce that all partners handle your data under strict confidentiality. We do not sell, trade, or rent client personal profiles to third-party marketing firms under any circumstances.

6. Your Legal Data Rights

As a global traveler, you hold absolute rights regarding how your personal information is handled by our platform:

  • Right of Access: You can request a digital copy of all personal details we hold associated with your name or email.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we update outdated passport details, incorrect phone numbers, or adjust dietary profiles.
  • Right to Erasure (Forgetfulness): You can request that we permanently delete your historical client record from our databases upon the conclusion of your tour.

To execute any of these rights, please submit a written request to our data protection coordinator at hello@kindredtravels.lk. We will process all verified requests within 14 business days.
