Payment Information

Supported payment methods, deposit schedules, currency exchanges, and security.

Last Updated: August 8, 2026

1. Supported Payment Methods

Kindred Travels provides three primary channels for processing booking payments:

  • Credit & Debit Cards: We accept Visa, MasterCard, and American Express. Card payments are executed via a secure, 3D-secure international merchant payment gateway. A standard **2.5% processing fee** is added to card transactions to cover administrative routing fees.
  • International Telegraphic Transfer (Bank Wire): Direct bank transfers are accepted in USD or EUR to our local commercial bank accounts in Colombo, Sri Lanka. Complete SWIFT codes, IBAN numbers, and routing addresses are provided with your proforma invoice. Sending bank charges and intermediary clearing fees must be paid in full by the sender.
  • Wise (formerly TransferWise): Clients can pay using Wise directly to our local bank account. Wise is our recommended transfer route as it offers the lowest conversion fees and near-instant processing.

2. Payment & Deposit Schedules

To prevent booking drops or room allocation releases, you must stick strictly to the following payment deadlines:

  • Initial Payment: The 30% non-refundable booking deposit is due within seven (7) business days of your approval of the final travel itinerary proposal.
  • Final Balance: The remaining 70% balance must be paid in full exactly thirty (30) days prior to your scheduled tour start date.
  • Late Balances: If balance payments are not completed 30 days prior to arrival, Kindred Travels reserves the right to treat the booking as cancelled by the client. The standard cancellation charges will apply.

3. Currency Exchange & Conversions

All custom tour packages and accommodation prices are quoted in United States Dollars (USD) or Euros (EUR).

If you wish to make bank transfer payments in local Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) or another major currency, you must request a quote calculation. The conversion rate applied is determined on the morning of your payment date using the official daily buying and selling exchange rates published by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL). We do not charge additional currency margins.

4. Security Protocols

Kindred Travels prioritizes your financial data security. We protect your billing information through the following standards:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All card payments are routed through a 256-bit SSL encrypted gateway.
  • PCI Compliance: Kindred Travels does not store, see, or retain your credit card numbers, CVV security codes, or banking passwords. Transactions are processed via tokenized methods hosted entirely by our gateway providers.
  • Fraud Prevention: The gateway utilizes automated 3D-Secure 2.0 validation checks (e.g. Verified by Visa or Mastercard Identity Check) to ensure secure authentication.

5. Local Tipping & Cash Guidelines

While card payments are widely accepted in Colombo, Galle Fort, Ella, and large resorts, cash is essential for smaller expenses in regional Sri Lanka.

We recommend holding a reasonable amount of local Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) cash for dining at local tea shops, purchasing roadside king coconuts, purchasing local crafts, and tipping your chauffeur-guides or safari jeep drivers. ATMs accepting international Visa and Mastercard are easily accessible in all main towns.
