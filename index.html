    Curated Travel Itineraries

    Select one of our signature packages tailored by local guides, or use our planner below to customize your dream route.

    Classic Ceylon Highlights

    A perfect introductory loop taking you through the cultural core, scenic hill country, and historic colonial coasts of the island.

    7 Days / 6 Nights
    Ideal for First-timers
    Easy - Moderate
    Day 1

    Arrival in Colombo

    Welcome to Sri Lanka! Transfer from the airport to your boutique city hotel. Enjoy a evening sunset walk along Galle Face Green.

    Day 2

    Sigiriya & Dambulla Caves

    Journey north. Stop to explore the massive gilded cave temples of Dambulla, then check in to your jungle lodge near Sigiriya.

    Day 3

    Sigiriya Fortress & Kandy

    Climb the iconic Lion Rock at sunrise for breathtaking 360-degree views. In the afternoon, transfer to the royal hill capital, Kandy.

    Day 4

    Royal Botanicals & Tea Country

    Visit Kandy’s Temple of the Tooth. Board the scenic train to the highlands and watch the landscape change into emerald rolling tea hills.

    Day 5

    Colonial Galle Fort

    Travel down to the south-west coast. Explore Galle Fort, walking its cobblestone lanes, lighthouses, and fortified walls.

    Day 6

    Beach Relaxation in Mirissa

    Spend a lazy day on the sand, go whale watching, or try your hand at surfing the mellow waves of Weligama beach.

    Day 7

    Departure from Colombo

    Take a final morning walk, collect souvenirs, and transfer back to Bandaranaike International Airport for your flight home.

    Highlands & Wildlife Quest

    Deepen your connection with Sri Lanka’s raw wilderness, tracking leopards in Yala, spotting elephants, and trekking cloud forests.

    10 Days / 9 Nights
    For Nature Lovers
    Moderate
    Day 1

    Arrival & Negombo Beach

    Arrive at the airport and check in at a coastal resort in Negombo. Rest up after your flight.

    Day 2

    Minneriya Elephant Gathering

    Drive to the north-central dry zone. Take an afternoon 4x4 safari in Minneriya to see hundreds of elephants by the reservoir.

    Day 3

    Sigiriya Climb & Hiriwadunna

    Conquer the 1,200 steps of Sigiriya. Afterwards, enjoy a traditional village catamaran ride and rural clay-pot lunch.

    Day 4

    Spices of Matale & Kandy

    Explore an organic spice garden on your way to Kandy. Experience a vibrant traditional Kandyan dance show in the evening.

    Day 5

    Trek Horton Plains & Nuwara Eliya

    Rise early for a dramatic hike in Horton Plains National Park to see World’s End cliff, dropping 880m into the abyss.

    Day 6

    Scenic Train to Ella

    Take the scenic blue train to Ella. Climb Little Adam’s Peak for sunset views over the famous Ella Gap.

    Day 7

    Leopard Country Safari (Yala)

    Travel down the mountains to Yala. Embark on a late afternoon jeep safari to seek out the elusive Sri Lankan leopards.

    Day 8

    Elephant Transit Home & Galle

    Visit the Udawalawe Elephant Transit Home to watch baby elephant feeding time. Proceed to Galle Fort to explore Dutch colonial heritage.

    Day 9

    Whale Expedition & Mirissa Reefs

    Board a morning boat to search for giant Blue Whales in the deep ocean, followed by snorkeling in sea turtle areas.

    Day 10

    Galle to Colombo Airport

    Transfer to Colombo via the southern expressway, reflecting on ten days of wild adventure as you catch your flight.

    Grand Ceylon Discovery

    The ultimate journey. Spans the ancient cultural ruins, the misty mountain hiking peaks, eastern coast beaches, safaris, and wellness spas.

    14 Days / 13 Nights
    Complete Island Experience
    Moderate - High
    Day 1

    Arrival & Welcome Dinner

    Land in Colombo. Meet your private chauffeur-guide and settle into your premium hotel. Enjoy a welcome dinner.

    Day 2

    Anuradhapura Ancient Kingdom

    Drive north to Anuradhapura, the island’s oldest royal capital dating back to the 4th century BC. See sacred ancient ruins.

    Day 3

    Sigiriya Rock & Clay Pots

    Climb the fortress of Sigiriya, then learn to cook authentic Sri Lankan curries at a local homestead.

    Day 4

    Polonnaruwa Medieval Ruins

    Rent a bicycle to explore the archaeological ruins, grand stone carvings, and giant water pools of Polonnaruwa.

    Day 5

    Trincomalee Beaches (East Coast)

    Drive east to Trincomalee. Settle into a beach resort on the white sands of Nilaveli. Rest and relax by the ocean.

    Day 6

    Snorkeling Pigeon Island

    Take a boat to Pigeon Island Marine National Park to snorkel with blacktip reef sharks, green sea turtles, and colorful corals.

    Day 7

    Dambulla Caves & Kandy Hills

    Travel south-west to Kandy, stopping at Dambulla Cave Temple. Settle into Kandy and attend a sacred evening ceremony.

    Day 8

    Temple of the Tooth & Botanical Gardens

    Tour Kandy’s historic sites and stroll the Peradeniya Botanical Gardens, famous for orchid houses and giant palms.

    Day 9

    Scenic Mountain Train Ride

    Board the train to Ella, traveling through tunnels, valleys, tea fields, and cloud forests.

    Day 10

    Trekking Ella Peak

    Embark on a challenging trek up Ella Peak for panoramic views. Visit the Nine Arch Bridge to watch the train cross.

    Day 11

    Udawalawe Elephant Sanctuary

    Journey to Udawalawe. Take a safari to see large herds of wild elephants drinking and bathing in the lake.

    Day 12

    Galle Fort & Colonial Streets

    Drive to the historical coastal fort of Galle. Settle in, explore, and dine on premium fresh seafood.

    Day 13

    Coastal Spa & Turtle Hatchery

    Unwind with an authentic Ayurvedic oil massage, visit a sea turtle hatchery, and relax on the gold sands of Bentota.

    Day 14

    Colombo City Tour & Departure

    A driving tour of Colombo’s Dutch Hospital shopping district and colonial landmarks before driving to the airport.

    Sri Lanka Travel Guide

    Everything you need to know before packing your bags. Read our travel checklist to ensure a seamless tropical getaway.

    Most tourists need an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) to enter Sri Lanka. You can easily apply online before traveling. Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date, and carry proof of a return flight ticket and sufficient travel funds.
    Sri Lanka has a tropical climate governed by two monsoon seasons. The southwest coast beaches and central hills are best from November to April, while the northeast coast (like Trincomalee) is sunny and dry from May to September. You can travel year-round because one side is always dry!
    The official currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). While luxury hotels and restaurants accept Visa and Mastercard, small vendors, tuk-tuks, and local guides only accept cash. ATMs are widely available in cities. Tipping (approx. 10%) is highly appreciated by drivers, guides, and hotel staff.
    Sri Lankans are incredibly warm and hospitable. When visiting Buddhist and Hindu temples, always cover your shoulders and knees, and remove your footwear and hats before entering. Avoid taking photos with your back turned directly to a Buddha statue, as it is considered disrespectful. The traditional greeting is "Ayubowan" (meaning "May you live long"), said with palms pressed together at chest level.
    Pack light, breathable cotton or linen clothing for the hot coastal areas, but bring a warm sweater or light jacket for the cooler Highlands (like Nuwara Eliya, where temps can drop to 12°C). Slip-on shoes are ideal for temple visits, along with sun protection (hat, sunscreen) and high-quality insect repellent for safaris.
